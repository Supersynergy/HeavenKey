The word list is included unchanged. HeavenKey selects words independently with
platform cryptographic randomness. Capitalization, separators and a compatibility
suffix are applied to generated results, not to the source list. Six words give
about 77.5 bits of word-choice entropy; eight give about 103.4 bits. These figures
assume independent uniform selection. Personal sentences do not have this guarantee.
